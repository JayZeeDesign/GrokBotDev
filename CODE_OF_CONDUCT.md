# Code of Conduct

grokbot.dev is a small, friendly, contribution-driven project. We want it to stay welcoming.

## The short version
- Be kind and assume good faith. Critique entries, not people.
- Keep it real: submissions must come from real sources and work (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`, G1–G8).
- No spam, no ads, no self-promotion dressed up as a use case (G6).
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks — in issues, PRs, or reviews.

## Scope
Applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, close, or reject any contribution that breaks these rules, and may block
repeat offenders. Report problems by opening an issue or emailing **hello@grokbot.dev**. Reports
are handled discreetly.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
