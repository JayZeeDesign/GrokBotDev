Trello works well for small teams that want Bot-visible tasks without a heavier issue tracker. A Grok Bot can create a card from an email, move a checklist item after a human approval, summarize stale cards, or turn a meeting note into a board update. Trello setups should be specific because card movement can hide real decisions. Good setups name the board, list, card fields, and movement rules. The Bot should keep the board tidy; it should not hide a decision behind a drag-and-drop move.
