Google Sheets is the practical place for numbers a Bot should not hide. It can update a weekly P&L, keep a sourcing list, refresh analytics, or turn a messy export into a small dashboard. Reporting stacks, one-person-company dashboards, and finance workflows fit this pattern. This hub is for Sheets entries where the Bot writes rows, formulas, summaries, or checks. Strong setups state the tab, the columns, the data source, and the sentence the Bot adds next to the numbers so a human understands the change.
