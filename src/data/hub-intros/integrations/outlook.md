Outlook is the same hard problem as Gmail with different office walls: private mail, calendar clues, attachments, and reply pressure. A Grok Bot can triage messages, draft follow-ups, pull meeting context, or summarize an inbox for a manager. Email-outreach and finance patterns transfer well here, with the same rule: sort first, send only after review. Good Outlook entries should name the mailbox or folder, keep sends approval-gated, and show how attachments are handled. The Bot's first job is to reduce the pile into decisions, not act like it owns the conversation.
