Salesforce is useful when the Bot treats it as a system of record, not a sandbox. It can propose field updates, summarize account history, find stale opportunities, or prepare a pipeline note from email and call context. Sales entries should keep writes gated because a small CRM change can confuse a team. This hub is for Salesforce-connected Grok Bots with clear objects and permissions. Good setups say whether the Bot touches Leads, Contacts, Opportunities, or Tasks, which fields it may edit, and what source event justified each proposed change.
