Telegram can be a fast notification surface for a Grok Bot, especially for personal alerts, ops notes, or lightweight support. Telegram works best as a focused command and alert surface, not a second inbox with no rules. Useful entries will keep the job narrow: send an alert, collect a command, summarize a daily check, or route one support request. Good setups name the chat, bot token storage, allowed users, and what happens when the Bot is unsure. Speed is nice; permissions matter more.
