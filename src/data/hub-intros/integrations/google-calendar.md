Google Calendar gives a Bot the shape of the day. It can prepare a morning brief, propose meeting windows, remind you about a task before the slot closes, or line up family logistics. The strongest scheduling setups are not just calendar edits; they put the right work in front of someone at the right time. This hub is for calendar-linked Bots with clear boundaries. Good entries say which calendars are visible, whether the Bot can create events, and how conflicts or private blocks are handled.
