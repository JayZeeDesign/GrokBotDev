Shopify Bots should start with simple store ops where the action boundary is obvious. A Grok Bot can watch orders, draft customer replies, check product copy, summarize returns, or surface inventory problems for a human. Marketing, support, and finance patterns all meet here. Good Shopify setups should name the store area, the data read, and the action boundary. Drafting a product description is low risk. Changing prices, refunds, inventory, or fulfillment needs a clear approval step and a source trail.
