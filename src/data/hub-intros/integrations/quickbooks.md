QuickBooks is a good finance source for Bots that summarize instead of decide. A Grok Bot can prepare a weekly P&L note, surface uncategorized transactions, match invoices, or export a clean view into Sheets for review. Finance-ops workflows around recurring invoices, reporting, and back-office paperwork fit this lane. This hub is for QuickBooks setups with clear read and write boundaries. Strong entries name the company file, report, account, and date range. The Bot should make the numbers easier to inspect, then leave final accounting choices to a person.
