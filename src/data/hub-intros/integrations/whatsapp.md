WhatsApp is personal and high-trust, so Grok Bot setups here need a stricter bar. A Bot might draft a reply, summarize a family thread, route a customer inquiry, or prepare a reminder for a small business. WhatsApp setups should borrow the safest message-bridge habit: preview first, then let a person send. This hub should prefer preview-first workflows and human sends. Good entries say which chats are in scope, whether messages are read-only, and how consent is handled. The Bot can help write; it should not surprise people from your number.
