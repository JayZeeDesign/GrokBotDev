Discord is a natural home for community, support, and builder crews, but it needs careful routing. The Discord Grok Bot Bridge draft shows a real path: a private server, allowlists, a bridge, and Bot replies returning to Discord. This hub is for setups that send digests, answer community questions, collect bug reports, or route work from a Discord channel. Strong entries name the server, channel, roles, and send rule. A Bot in Discord should be easy to interrupt, easy to audit, and quiet by default.
