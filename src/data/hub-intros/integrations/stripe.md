Stripe is a money integration, so the Bot should be careful and boring. It can summarize subscriptions, surface failed payments, prepare invoice reminders, or match customer email to billing history. The right billing pattern is draft, check, and route before money moves. This hub is for Stripe setups with explicit scopes. Strong entries say whether the Bot is read-only, what object it reads, and which actions require approval. A good billing Bot makes the account easier to understand. It should not quietly refund, cancel, or charge.
