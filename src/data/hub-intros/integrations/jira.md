Jira setups need discipline because the board can become its own weather system. A Grok Bot can summarize stale tickets, draft acceptance criteria, link a commit to an issue, or prepare a release note from closed work. This hub is for Jira entries that make the board clearer without hiding decisions. Good setups name the project, issue type, status transitions, and who approves movement. The Bot should bring evidence from commits, comments, tests, or docs. It should not drag cards across columns just because the wording sounds done.
