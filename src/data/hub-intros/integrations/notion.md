Notion is a good landing place for Bot memory because the table stays visible. Other setups can store swipefiles, competitor deltas, support patterns, or personal study notes. This hub is for Notion databases, pages, and lightweight operating systems. Good entries name the database, the properties the Bot writes, and how duplicates are handled. A table full of sources beats a polished answer you cannot audit.
