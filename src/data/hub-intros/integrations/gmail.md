Gmail is the inbox version of real work: messy, private, and full of half-decisions. A Grok Bot can triage threads, draft replies, pull invoices, sort outreach responses, or build a morning brief from overnight mail. The best email setups keep sends gated and focus on sorting before writing. This integration hub is for setups that use Gmail as a source, a draft surface, or a careful send path. Strong entries say which labels or senders the Bot can read, whether drafts stay unsent, and how a person approves anything leaving the account.
