Airtable is useful when a team wants structure without a heavy app. A Grok Bot can update a content calendar, lead table, research backlog, bug triage list, or lightweight CRM. Airtable works best when the base has a clear job: track content, leads, research, bugs, or a lightweight CRM. Good setups name the base, table, fields, and duplicate rule. The Bot should write small, auditable records and attach the source. If the table becomes a dumping ground, the setup needs a narrower job.
