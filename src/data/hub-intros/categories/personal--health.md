Health is a sensitive lane, so the bar is narrow. A Grok Bot can help organize workout notes, summarize a meal plan, remind you about a routine, or prepare questions for a professional. It should not diagnose, prescribe, or pretend a chat summary is medical advice. The useful version stays narrow: organize notes, prepare questions, and keep reminders separate from medical judgment. Useful entries will be plain about scope: what the Bot reads, what it reminds you to do, and when it points you back to a clinician, coach, or other qualified person.
