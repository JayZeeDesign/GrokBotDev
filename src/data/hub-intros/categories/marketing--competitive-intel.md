Competitive intel gets noisy fast, so the best Bot here reports deltas. A strong competitor watch has one rule: tell me what changed, not every old fact again. A Grok Bot can watch competitor pages, X posts, app-store listings, ads, pricing pages, or release notes, then summarize only the movement. This hub is for those watchlists and evidence trails. The useful entries show the source, the old state, the new state, and the confidence level. If the Bot cannot prove a claim, it should say that plainly and leave the conclusion alone.
