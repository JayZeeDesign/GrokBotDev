Travel planning is a bundle of small decisions: dates, weather, reservations, documents, delays, and who needs to know. A Grok Bot can watch a trip folder, draft a day plan, check confirmation emails, or build a packing list from the calendar. The safest travel setup sticks to simple, reviewable work: gather facts, draft options, and ask before changing plans. Good entries will name the sources, keep private documents scoped, and make suggestions rather than bookings. A Bot can reduce travel friction; it should not spend money or change plans without approval.
