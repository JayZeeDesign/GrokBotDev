Finance reporting should end with a short story about the numbers. A Grok Bot can pull QuickBooks data into Sheets, compare this week to last week, and write a plain P&L note for a founder or operator. Weekly reporting, daily analytics, and small dashboards for one-person companies all fit this lane. This hub is for reports that are meant to be read, not admired. Strong entries name the source, the date range, the formula or metric, and the one decision the report is supposed to make easier.
