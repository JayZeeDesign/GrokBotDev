Escalation is not a failure of automation. It is the safety rail. A Grok Bot can identify refund risk, legal language, angry customers, security issues, or any case that needs a manager, founder, or specialist. This hub is for setups that route the hard ticket quickly and leave a useful summary behind. Docs, outreach, and engineering setups all point to the same lesson: hard calls need a clear human gate. A strong escalation entry names the trigger words, the data checked, the destination channel, and what the Bot is forbidden to resolve alone.
