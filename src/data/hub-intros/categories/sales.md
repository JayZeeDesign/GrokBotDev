Sales work rewards Bots that are patient, narrow, and honest about approval. The useful setups do not pretend to close deals alone. They find likely accounts, enrich a lead list, draft the first note, update the CRM, or summarize what changed in a pipeline. Strong sales setups include prospect research, email outreach with before-and-after sorting, and small teams where Scout or Pitch has a defined lane. Use this category for sales Bots that reduce follow-up drag while keeping the human relationship intact. Good entries name the data source, the CRM field, and the moment a person reads before send.
