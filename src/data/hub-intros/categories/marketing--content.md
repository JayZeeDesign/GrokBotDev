Content work becomes useful when each Bot has a lane. A full production crew can split research, writing, visuals, analysis, scheduling, and publishing into separate Bot lanes. Another agency stack watched trends, kept a swipefile, and handled recurring content QA. This hub is for those repeatable loops, not one-shot blog fluff. A strong entry says where ideas come from, what draft shape the Bot produces, how facts are checked, and who approves publishing. The point is not more content. It is fewer cold starts, cleaner handoffs, and drafts that arrive with sources still attached.
