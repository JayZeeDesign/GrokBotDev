Research is one of the richest Grok Bot lanes because the receipts can stay attached. The simple version scans X or the web, saves candidates into Notion, and sends a short digest. The advanced version fans out across sources, removes duplicates, and runs a verifier before writing a summary. A factored digest adds another good habit: label each source by confidence before the synthesis. Use this hub when you need a Bot to gather, filter, and explain, not just answer. Better research setups show source links, what was rejected, and where uncertainty survived.
