Triage is the first useful support job because it does not require the Bot to be right about the final answer. It can label urgency, group duplicates, pull account context, and send a short summary to the right person. In broader startup and agency crews, support triage keeps the queue from becoming one big pile. This hub is for that sorting layer. Good entries show the labels, the source fields, the confidence threshold, and what happens when a customer sounds angry, confused, or blocked.
