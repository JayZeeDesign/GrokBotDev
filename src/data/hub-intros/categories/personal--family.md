Family Bots need warmth and restraint. They can help with shared calendars, school emails, trip logistics, grocery reminders, or a message draft that needs a kinder first pass. The iMessage skill draft shows the right caution: local access, preview-first sending, and a native confirmation before a message leaves. This hub is for family admin that reduces mental load without handing over judgment. Strong entries name the account, the people involved, and the send rule. A Bot can prepare, remind, and summarize. A person should decide anything emotionally loaded.
