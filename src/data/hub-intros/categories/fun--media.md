Media Bots can help gather, draft, trim, caption, and package creative work. The content-production and agency stacks include visual, GIF, and publishing roles; a short video can also be the receipt. This hub is for setups around images, clips, scripts, thumbnails, captions, and swipefiles. A strong entry names the source asset, the output format, and the review step before anything is posted. The Bot can speed up the rough cut or the first caption. It should not claim rights, likeness, or facts it does not have.
