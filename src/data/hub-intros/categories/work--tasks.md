Task Bots are useful when they keep ownership visible. They can collect the loose ends from mail, chat, docs, and repos, then sort each item into do now, decide, delegate, or ignore. Several chief-of-staff patterns fit here: Firstmate routes work to other Bots, Atlas uses a hub-and-specialist setup, and solo operators lean on scheduled checklists. This hub is for that practical middle layer. Look for entries with clear states, short task names, and a human approval point when the Bot wants to change a deadline, assign work, or close the loop.
