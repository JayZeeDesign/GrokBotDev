Work is where a Grok Bot earns trust or loses it fast. The useful setups do small, named jobs: sort the overnight inbox, draft the brief before a meeting, keep a task list honest, or turn a messy research trail into a readable note. Good work setups share that shape: chief-of-staff Bots, daily research tables, docs that update after a merge, and crews that hand work back for approval. This hub keeps the work lane practical. Look for setups that say what they sign into, what they change, and where a human stays in the loop.
