Crypto alerts are useful only when the rule is clear before the ping. A Grok Bot can watch open interest, funding, volume, liquidation clusters, or whale flow, then send a short note when the setup matches. A strong crypto monitor uses a bounce score and an alert gate, plus a bearish view to keep the signal honest. This hub is for alerts with context: threshold, time window, source, and counterpoint. A good Bot wakes you up to inspect a condition. It does not tell you the trade is safe.
