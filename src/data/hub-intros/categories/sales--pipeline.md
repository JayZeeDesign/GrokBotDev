Pipeline Bots are best when they explain movement. They can spot deals with no next step, gather blockers from email or CRM notes, and draft a short weekly view of what changed. In the Atlas-style crews, sales work is split: one Bot researches, another drafts outreach, and the hub keeps decisions visible. This hub is for that coordination layer. Look for setups that show stage changes, stale opportunities, owner gaps, and risk notes in plain language. If a Bot moves a deal, the entry should also show the source event that justified it.
