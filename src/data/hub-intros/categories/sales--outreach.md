Outreach is high leverage and easy to ruin. The strongest pattern here is not a fantasy sequence; it is a live email workflow with daily reports and reply sorting. That is the bar. A Grok Bot can draft variants, group replies, surface objections, and keep the human focused on the conversations worth having. This hub is for careful Gmail, Outlook, HubSpot, or Salesforce outreach setups. The good ones limit who can be contacted, show the message before send, and treat positive replies as a handoff, not a chance to improvise.
