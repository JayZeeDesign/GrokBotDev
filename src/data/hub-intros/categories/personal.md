Personal Bots are allowed to be useful, funny, and small. Good examples make that clear: a morning surf report, a Chrome extension that blocks procrastination with a joke, family-style calendar work, and message bridges with strong send gates. This category is not lesser than work. It is where a Grok Bot can remove tiny daily frictions without a meeting, a dashboard, or a CRM. The best personal setups are specific: one route, one inbox, one household rhythm, one habit. They also respect privacy. A good entry says what the Bot can see and when it asks.
