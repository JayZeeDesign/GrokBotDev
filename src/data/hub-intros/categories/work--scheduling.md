Scheduling sounds small until it becomes the hidden job under every other job. A Grok Bot can watch a calendar, propose windows, prepare recurring checks, and nudge a task at the right moment. The best setups are not just calendar math: a self-scheduling monitor desk can run its own scans, a surf report can arrive before the morning decision, and crews can use routines to keep work moving. Use this hub for setups that respect time as a boundary. The Bot should make the next slot easier to choose, not fill your day with clever noise.
