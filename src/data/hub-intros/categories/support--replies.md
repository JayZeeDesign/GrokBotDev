Reply drafting is helpful only when the Bot knows when to stop. It can turn a known answer into a friendly response, quote the right policy, or ask for missing details. Discord and iMessage bridges make the risk plain: messages feel personal, and a bad send lands instantly. This hub is for support reply setups with previews, approval gates, and source links. Strong entries show the knowledge source, the customer context, the draft, and the point where a human says yes. The Bot should reduce blank-page time, not become the support voice alone.
