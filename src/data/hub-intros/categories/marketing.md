Marketing Bots earn their keep by turning noisy inputs into a small number of useful choices. Strong marketing setups include content crews, agency trend monitors, app-store growth work, and competitor-intel Bots that report only what changed. This category covers research, content, social, ads, analytics, SEO, and positioning. The best setups are not generic content machines. They watch a lane, gather receipts, draft with constraints, and leave the final taste decision to a person. Use this hub when the Bot is helping you pick what to say, where to say it, and what changed after you did.
