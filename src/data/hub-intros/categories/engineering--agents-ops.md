Bot operations is the lane for keeping a crew sane. The public examples are rich: Firstmate creates and routes work, Grok Ship keeps local state, an eight-Bot fleet records handoffs and interrupts, and a meta-prompt can turn a proven workflow into a reusable team setup. This hub is for memory, routing, wake-ups, handoffs, guardrails, and lessons learned. Good entries should make the invisible parts visible: where state lives, how Bots message each other, who owns a task, and what stops a loop before it burns time.
