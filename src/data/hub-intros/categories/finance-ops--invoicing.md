Invoicing is a good Bot job because the steps are repetitive and the stakes are clear. A setup can check a purchase order, draft an invoice, send a reminder, or surface a mismatch before anyone chases the wrong customer. Recurring invoices with PO matching are a clean pattern; invoice-chaser setups add the reminder layer. This hub is for Stripe, Gmail, QuickBooks, and spreadsheet flows around money owed. Good entries show the invoice source, the approval rule, and exactly what the Bot may send on its own.
