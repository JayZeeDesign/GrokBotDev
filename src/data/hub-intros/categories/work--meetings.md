Meeting work starts before the call and ends after everyone forgets the action items. The best Bot setup does not replace the room. It gathers context, pulls open questions, prepares a short brief, and turns the notes into owner-and-date tasks when the call ends. The strongest meeting patterns stay plain: agenda prep, follow-up drafts, decision logs, and calendar-linked reminders. A good meeting Bot should make the next conversation sharper. It should not invent consensus or send commitments without a person reading them first.
