Dashboards help when they answer a question before they decorate a wall. A Grok Bot can refresh a Notion table, update Google Sheets, summarize Search Console, or maintain a small operator dashboard with counts that matter. One-person company dashboards, X-to-Notion research tables, and weekly reporting patterns belong here. This hub is for dashboards with a job: show what changed, what broke, or what needs a decision. Strong entries state the data source, the refresh rule, and the exact sentence the Bot writes next to the numbers.
