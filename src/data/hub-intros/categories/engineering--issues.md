Issue work is small enough for a Bot and important enough to track. The good setups verify a commit, open or close the matching issue, attach evidence, and leave a clean status note. Grok Ship also shows how routines can keep issue triage moving at larger scale. This hub is for GitHub, Linear, Jira, and board-style issue flows. A useful entry shows what counts as done, how duplicates are handled, and when the Bot asks for help. Closing an issue should never be a vibe; it needs a commit, a test, or a human note.
