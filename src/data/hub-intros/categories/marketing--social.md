Social Bots should listen before they post. They can watch X for a topic, collect examples, draft a thread, turn one asset into several short posts, or tell you which replies deserve a human. The best setups use social as a signal source: AI-tool radar, X-to-Notion research, trend spotting, and content repurposing. This hub is for setups that make the timeline easier to work with. Favor Bots that cite the posts they found, separate inspiration from copying, and treat posting as an approval-gated step, not a background surprise.
