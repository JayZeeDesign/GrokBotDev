Enrichment is where raw rows become usable work. A Grok Bot can take a lead, company, app, repo, or support ticket and add missing context from public sources or connected tools. Prospect research can pull from X, Reddit, and LinkedIn; app-growth work adds store and competitor data. This hub is for enrichment that stays grounded. Good entries show the original record, the sources checked, the fields added, and the confidence level. A Bot should leave blanks when the evidence is not there, not fill a table with guesses.
