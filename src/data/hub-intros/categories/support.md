Support Bots need a narrow job and a human escape hatch. They can triage tickets, draft replies, update a knowledge base, group similar complaints, or surface the case that needs a person now. Startup support roles, Intercom doc updates, and Discord/iMessage bridges show how careful messaging tools must be. This category is for support work that improves response quality without pretending every customer problem is routine. Look for entries that name the queue, the draft policy, the escalation rule, and the evidence a Bot uses before it touches a reply.
