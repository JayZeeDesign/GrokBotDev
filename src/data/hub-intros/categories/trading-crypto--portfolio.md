Portfolio Bots should help you understand exposure, not push trades. They can summarize positions, note concentration, watch cost basis, or prepare a weekly view of what changed. Portfolio setups should stay read-only until the user has checked the source account, tracker, and risk note. The best entries should name the exchange, wallet, sheet, or tracker, explain whether the Bot is read-only, and separate accounting from action. A Bot can make the current state visible. It should not rebalance, sell, or chase yield without a human decision.
