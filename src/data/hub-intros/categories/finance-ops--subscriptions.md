Subscriptions hide in plain sight. A Grok Bot can scan Stripe, email receipts, card charges, or a shared spreadsheet to find renewals, failed payments, plan changes, and tools nobody uses anymore. The safest subscription setup stays practical: read the billing source, explain the renewal, and draft the next step for review. Good entries should say what account is read, what counts as a renewal risk, and whether the Bot can only draft a cancellation or reminder. Money leaves the company quietly; the Bot's job is to make that quiet list visible.
