Personal money Bots should make the picture clearer, not make decisions for you. They can group subscriptions, summarize bills, prepare a budget note, or pull a weekly spending view into Sheets. Finance-ops setups offer useful patterns: invoice checks, QuickBooks-style reporting, and subscription visibility. This hub is for personal versions of those flows. Good entries state the account read scope, avoid investment advice, and keep payment or cancellation steps approval-gated. The Bot's job is to show the list and the tradeoff, then let you choose.
