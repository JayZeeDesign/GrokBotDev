Game Bots work when the loop is small enough to play and clear enough to repeat. Useful setups might create a party-game host, a puzzle generator, a tabletop NPC helper, or a scoreboard that updates from a chat. The important thing is a playable loop, not a vague promise that the Bot is entertaining. Good entries should include rules, limits, example turns, and what the Bot does when players disagree. Treat the best setups as concrete builds: small games, clear outputs, and no pretending a demo is a product.
