Alerts are only useful when they are rare enough to trust. A Grok Bot can watch a metric, a repo event, a market condition, or a customer queue, then ping the right place when a threshold is crossed. A crypto monitor can use a scored bounce alert; engineering workflows can use status updates; agency stacks can send digest signals into chat. This hub is for alerting that comes with context. A good entry names the threshold, the destination, the fallback path, and what evidence is included. No one needs another noisy siren with no source attached.
