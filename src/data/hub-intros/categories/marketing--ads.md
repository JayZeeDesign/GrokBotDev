Ad work needs guardrails because small changes spend real money. A useful Grok Bot can monitor a swipefile, summarize competitor angles, draft creative variants, or pull metrics from a connected ads account for review. App-growth work can combine ASO, ads, social, and store data in one loop; agency stacks can also keep a daily ad swipefile. This hub is for those careful systems. Good entries should name which account is read-only, which actions are draft-only, and who approves spend, targeting, copy, or store changes.
