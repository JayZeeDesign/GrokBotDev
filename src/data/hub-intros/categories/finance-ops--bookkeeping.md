Bookkeeping Bots should collect and explain, not freelance your books. A good setup can gather receipts, match a transaction to an invoice, prepare a category suggestion, and send the summary to QuickBooks or a sheet for review. Back-office work often has this texture: purchase orders, maintenance records, depreciation, and recurring paperwork. This hub is for safe bookkeeping helpers. Strong entries state whether the Bot is read-only, which ledger or folder it reads, and how a human confirms categories before the record becomes official.
