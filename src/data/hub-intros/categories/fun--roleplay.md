Roleplay works when the character helps the job instead of hiding the job. People naturally name Bots — Firstmate, Atlas, Scout, Ledger — and some setups lean into persona for focus or fun. This hub is for character-driven Bots with a purpose: a writing partner, game host, rehearsal partner, or themed assistant. Roleplay setups need concrete proof because a character voice can make a vague tool feel more finished than it is. A good roleplay Bot says what it can do, how it stays in bounds, and when it drops the act for safety or clarity.
