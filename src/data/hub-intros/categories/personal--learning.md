Learning Bots are strongest when they build a trail you can revisit. They can gather sources, turn a topic into a short lesson, make flashcards from your own notes, or keep a research log with confidence tags. Factored digests and research swarms carry over nicely here: source labels, contradictions, and references matter when you are trying to learn, not just collect. This hub is for personal study setups that make curiosity durable. Good entries should show the reading list, the question, the summary, and a way to check where each claim came from.
