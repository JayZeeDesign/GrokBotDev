Email is a good first job because the mess is visible. A Grok Bot can read a narrow slice of mail, group replies by intent, draft the boring follow-ups, and leave the risky sends for you. The strongest source in the queue is live outreach: messages sorted, daily reports written, and replies routed without pretending the Bot should own the whole relationship. Use this hub for Gmail or Outlook setups that save attention, not ones that blast strangers. Good entries show the folder, the rule, and the approval step before anything leaves the inbox.
