Compliance Bots are not lawyers, accountants, or auditors. They are checklist keepers with receipts. A useful setup can verify required fields, collect evidence, surface missing approvals, and write a summary that points back to the source files. Finance, support, and engineering all need this pattern when a Bot touches customer data, money, or production systems. This hub is for guardrails: approval logs, access notes, billing records, and policy checks. Good entries avoid legal confidence theater. They show what was checked, what was missing, and who must make the decision.
