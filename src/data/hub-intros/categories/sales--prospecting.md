Prospecting starts as a research problem, not a send button. A Grok Bot can search a market, pull company clues, check fit against an ICP, and give you a short list that deserves human attention. Atlas had a Scout role for this exact shape, and the solo reporting stack included sourcing Bots across LinkedIn, Reddit, and X. This hub is for prospect lists, lead enrichment, account research, and first-pass qualification. The best entries show where the Bot looked, why a lead made the cut, and what it refused to guess when the evidence was thin.
