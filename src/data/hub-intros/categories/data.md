Data hubs are where Grok Bot turns scattered signals into something you can act on. Strong data setups include X-to-Notion research, AI-tool radar, a self-scheduling monitor desk, crypto market checks, reporting stacks, and dashboards for solo operators. This category is for scraping, monitoring, enrichment, alerts, and charts. The good Bot does not just gather rows. It explains where the data came from, what changed, and what should be checked next. If a setup cannot show source links or a repeatable rule, it belongs in a draft folder until the receipts improve.
