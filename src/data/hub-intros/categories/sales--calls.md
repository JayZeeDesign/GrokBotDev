Call work is about preparation and follow-through. A Grok Bot can pull account history, draft a short pre-call brief, turn messy notes into next steps, and remind you what was promised before the next conversation. The safest call setup stays close to source notes, account history, and plain follow-up drafts. Useful entries will name the source of notes, the CRM destination, and the approval rule for follow-up emails. A Bot can make the call cleaner. It should not pretend to understand tone or commitment without human review.
