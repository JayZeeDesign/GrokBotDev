Analytics Bots are at their best when they turn dashboards into decisions. They can pull yesterday's numbers, compare them to a baseline, and tell you which change deserves attention. Useful analytics setups include daily cross-product numbers, weekly Search Console reporting, app-growth metrics, and small operator dashboards. This hub is for setups that connect Google Sheets, QuickBooks, app stores, or product analytics into plain reports. Avoid mystery scores. A good entry says what changed, why it might matter, what source produced the number, and what the Bot recommends checking next.
