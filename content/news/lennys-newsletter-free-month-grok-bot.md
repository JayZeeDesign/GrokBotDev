Lenny Rachitsky says annual subscribers to Lenny's Newsletter can redeem a free month of Grok Bot, included in Cursor Pro+, through Lenny's Product Pass. If you already subscribe, go to [lennysproductpass.com](https://www.lennysproductpass.com/) and search for "Grok". If you are new, subscribe at [lennysnewsletter.com/subscribe](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/subscribe) and look for the Product Pass link in your welcome email.

The post says this is the first time SpaceXAI has offered a partner deal like this. Lenny also shared a few of his own favorite Grok Bot jobs, including support email triage, school calendar updates, podcast prep, Odyssey ticket hunting, and the [Be Happier bot](/use-cases/be-happier-bot/). The useful move is simple: redeem the month, hand Grok Bot one concrete routine, and see whether it earns a spot in your weekly system.
