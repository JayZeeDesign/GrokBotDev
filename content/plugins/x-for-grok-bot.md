## What it does

X for Grok Bot is the official first-party connector between your Bot and your X account. Connect once — Grok Bot spins up a developer account for you automatically, with included API credits — and your Bot can then search posts with advanced operators, read your timeline, mentions and likes, pull trending topics, look up any public account's recent posts, and manage your bookmarks.

It is read-and-research first: the high-value uses are summarising what moved in the topics you follow, finding accounts worth knowing, and turning a messy bookmarks pile into something you actually read. Posting and other write actions should stay behind your explicit approval.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Connect the plugin, then paste the prompt on this page. It sets your Bot up as a standing X desk: a morning summary of your beat, a few new accounts worth following based on who your network engages with, and your best bookmarks turned into a short reading list — all from real X data, and with a hard stop before anything is posted on your behalf.
