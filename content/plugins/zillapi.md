## What it does

ZillAPI gives builders fast, scalable data endpoints without standing up their own infrastructure. Connected to a Grok Bot, it becomes the bot’s way to fetch and enrich real data on demand, so answers are grounded in a live source instead of the model’s best guess.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Drop in the prompt below and a key. When you ask the bot for data, it pulls from ZillAPI and returns it cleanly — and refuses to make numbers up when the source has none.

Paste the prompt on this page into a Grok Bot and connect your account — that is the whole setup.
