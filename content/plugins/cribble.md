## What it does

Cribble is "ranking AI users, worldwide" - a quiet browser extension that tracks your sessions across 47 AI platforms (Grok, ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Gemini, Perplexity and more) and puts you on a global leaderboard. Sessions land on their own: you get a score, streaks, and a twelve-week heat trail, with scoring that rewards depth over volume - ten locked-in minutes outscore a hundred idle tabs. There are seasonal resets each quarter, 25 achievement medals (the Apex Legend is held only by the current #1), customizable pilot plates, and a lurk mode for privacy.

## Use it with Grok

Cribble is not a Grok Bot connector - it's a companion that measures your Grok (and wider AI) usage rather than adding tools to your Bot. Install the extension, sign in, and use Grok as you normally would; Cribble tracks it in the background and ranks you against everyone else on the board. Planned phases add squad competitions and CLI agent tracking for tools like Cursor and Claude Code. It's currently invite-only private beta.
