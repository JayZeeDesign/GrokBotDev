## What it does

Mentions.so is AI-search optimization: it tracks how your brand shows up when people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini and other models — sentiment, visibility against competitors, traffic from AI, and a board of recommended fixes. Pointed at your account, a Grok Bot can pull that picture on demand and tell you where you're losing the AI answer.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Paste the prompt on this page into a Grok Bot and connect it to your Mentions.so account. The bot reads Mentions.so's docs first, then reports your AI-search visibility, how it's trending, where competitors lead, and the top recommended actions — reporting only what Mentions.so actually returns, and never changing settings without your go-ahead.
