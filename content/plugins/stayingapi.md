## What it does

StayingAPI gives agents access to booking and accommodation sites through one simple API — search availability, pull listings, and compare prices across hotels and short-stay rentals without scraping. Connected to a Grok Bot, it means you can ask where to stay for your dates and get real, comparable options back in the chat.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Add the prompt below and a key. Ask about a place to stay for your dates and location; the bot searches booking sites through StayingAPI and returns the available options with prices to compare.

Paste the prompt on this page into a Grok Bot and connect your account — that is the whole setup.
