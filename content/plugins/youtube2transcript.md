## What it does

YouTube2Transcript pulls a clean, readable transcript from any YouTube video instantly. Wired into a Grok Bot, it means a link is enough: the bot reads the video, so you can summarize it, quote it, or research it without ever leaving the chat or watching the whole thing.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Add the prompt below. Share a YouTube link and the bot returns a clean transcript, ready to summarize or search, with the source link kept.

Paste the prompt on this page into a Grok Bot and connect your account — that is the whole setup.
