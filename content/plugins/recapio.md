## What it does

Recapio turns a meeting into a clean recap and a list of action items automatically. Connected to a Grok Bot, it means every call leaves behind a written summary and a split of who owns what — the standing job people used to keep in their own heads, done for them.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Add the prompt below. After each meeting the bot returns a recap that separates your commitments from theirs, one message per meeting.

Paste the prompt on this page into a Grok Bot and connect your account — that is the whole setup.
