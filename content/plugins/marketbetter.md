## What it does

MarketBetter turns website visits and buyer-intent signals into a prioritized daily SDR playbook: it identifies the companies on your site, builds and enriches prospect lists, drafts outreach from the signal context, and syncs with HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack and Teams. Its MCP server lets a Grok Bot run that GTM loop on demand.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Paste the prompt on this page into a Grok Bot and connect it to MarketBetter's MCP server or API. The bot reads MarketBetter's docs first, then each day it pulls the ranked playbook, explains the intent behind each account, and drafts the outreach — never sending a message, dialing, or writing to your CRM without your explicit go-ahead.
