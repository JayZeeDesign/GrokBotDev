## What it does

GenerateSpecs takes a rough idea and returns a structured, build-ready specification — problem statement, scope, milestones and acceptance criteria — instead of a vague brief. Wired into a Grok Bot, it turns "spec this out" into a real document your team or your coding agents can build from directly.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Add the prompt below and a key. Describe what you want built; the bot drafts it through GenerateSpecs, asks only for what is missing, and hands back a spec with a shareable link.

Paste the prompt on this page into a Grok Bot and connect your account — that is the whole setup.
