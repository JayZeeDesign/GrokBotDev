## What it does

TranscriptAPI turns any audio or video into an accurate transcript with a single API call — timestamps included, no pipeline to build. It is the fast way to give a Grok Bot the ability to read recordings, podcasts, meetings and videos, so anything spoken becomes text your bot can search, summarize and act on.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Give your bot the prompt below and an API key. From then on, dropping a recording link into the chat gets you a clean transcript back, and you can ask the bot to pull just the part around a timestamp.

Paste the prompt on this page into a Grok Bot and connect your account — that is the whole setup.
