## What it does

Amplemarket is "Step into the future of sales: Human + AI" - an all-in-one AI sales platform built around **Duo**, its AI sales agent. It covers the whole pipeline: a prospect database with powerful filters (Searcher), buying-intent signals (website visits, job changes, competitor mentions, social engagement), data enrichment, competitive intelligence, multichannel sequences with AI-personalized copy (Duo Copywriter and Duo Voice), a unified inbox, and deliverability tooling (domain health, email warmup, spam checking). Its Grok Bot connector puts that stack in your Bot's hands.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Add the Amplemarket connector in your Bot's settings and paste the prompt on this page. From then on you prospect in plain language - "find 20 VPs of sales at European fintechs", "enrich this person", "who changed jobs at my target accounts" - and your Bot researches, enriches and drafts sequences from real Amplemarket data. Because outreach reaches real people, the prompt keeps the Bot on drafts-only: it shows you exact recipients and messages and waits for your approval before anything sends or enrolls. Pairs naturally with X prospecting - see the "find customers complaining about competitors" use case.
