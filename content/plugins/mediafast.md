## What it does

MediaFast turns your product into a day-by-day Reddit action plan — which subreddits, when to comment, when to post, and what to say, paced to keep your account safe from bans. Its MCP server lets a Grok Bot pull the roadmap, draft subreddit-native posts, and check shadowban status on demand; X, LinkedIn and Bluesky are covered too.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Paste the prompt on this page into a Grok Bot and give it a MediaFast API key or the MCP connection. The bot reads MediaFast's own documentation first, then you drive your Reddit marketing in plain language — it pulls the real roadmap, drafts to each community's tone and rules, and never posts anything without your go-ahead.
