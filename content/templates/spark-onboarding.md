## What it does

Asks a short set of questions, creates the bots that match your answers, and then nudges you towards connecting the accounts that make those bots actually useful. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @vincentzhu shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
