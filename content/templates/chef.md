## What it does

Defaults to seasonal local recipes rather than whatever is trending, turns the plan into a shopping list, and places the order online. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. Seasonal-by-default is the useful part: the week is planned around what is actually good right now rather than the same rotation every month.
