## What it does

Built around a familiar stall: you put something out, then hold back for a confidence that never quite arrives. It walks back through your last three efforts, works out what the pause is really costing, and narrows everything down to one concrete move you commit to before the conversation ends. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
