## What it does

A look-but-do-not-touch helper for meeting people. It works through candidate connections and suggested introductions on your behalf and reports back on where your account stands, so what you get is an assessment to act on. It is deliberately confined to reading, and alters nothing held on your side. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @raily shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
