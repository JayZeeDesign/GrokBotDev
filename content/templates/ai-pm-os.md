## What it does

Built for product managers who keep rebuilding the same routine from scratch. Instead of a scattered pile of one-off prompts and tasks, it wires planning, prioritization and follow-through into a single standing setup, so the recurring parts of the job run from one place each week. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
