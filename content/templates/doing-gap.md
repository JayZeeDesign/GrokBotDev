## What it does

Aimed at the learner who has finished a dozen courses and released nothing. It tallies the material you have worked through beside the things you actually completed, puts words to the anxiety hiding behind the next lesson, and pushes you toward building rather than queuing up more study. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @thekuchh shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
