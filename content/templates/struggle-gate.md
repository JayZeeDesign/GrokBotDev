## What it does

A deliberately unhelpful first responder. Bring it a problem and it holds its explanation back for ten minutes, prompting you to have a genuine go at the thing yourself, so that when the guidance finally arrives it settles onto work you have already done instead of replacing it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
