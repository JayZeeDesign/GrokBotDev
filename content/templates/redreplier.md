## What it does

It sweeps four public forums - Hacker News, Reddit, Bluesky and X - for anyone naming your product, naming a rival, or otherwise sounding like they are close to buying something. Fresh hits arrive already sorted, so the conversations most likely to become a customer sit at the top. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
