## What it does

The classes worth going to fill within minutes of registration opening, which is usually while you are still asleep or already at work. This one waits on the reservation page for the session you asked for and takes the place as soon as the gym releases it, so the popular instructors stay within reach. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
