## What it does

Two messages, one at either end of the day. The first arrives with a line worth reading and then everything on the calendar; the second lands before bed and walks through tomorrow. Both carry the particulars that decide whether you are late: when exactly, and the road you are headed to. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
