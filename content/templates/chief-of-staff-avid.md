## What it does

One conversation covers both, and it locks down who you are and what the company is before it starts operating, rather than guessing from context. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. Locking down the basics before it operates is what stops it inventing context, which is the usual failure of a bot asked to run two things at once.
