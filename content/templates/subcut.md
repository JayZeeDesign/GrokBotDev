## What it does

Point it at your inbox and it hunts down every repeating payment buried in your receipts, then separates the ones billed every month from the ones billed once a year. It ranks which are worth dropping or moving to a cheaper tier and hands you that shortlist. Nothing is cancelled for you; the final call stays yours. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
