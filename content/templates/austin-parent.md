## What it does

Built for Austin households where term dates, activity sign-ups and the rest of the family diary all arrive in fragments. It keeps those threads together in one place and reads every fresh notice, message or reminder as a job with an owner and a due date, so the next thing to handle is never buried in an inbox. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
