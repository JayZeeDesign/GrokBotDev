## What it does

Built for one narrow search: work in AI trust, safety and child protection. It keeps an eye out for postings as they appear, points you toward the people worth knowing in that field, and helps you open a real conversation on LinkedIn instead of firing off another cold application. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
