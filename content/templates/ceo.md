## What it does

Holds the top-level view of the business: strategy, what gets priority, how it makes money and how it reaches people. Rather than doing the work itself it studies what is going on, decides what matters next, and puts specialist bots on the individual pieces. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
