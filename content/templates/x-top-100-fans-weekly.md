## What it does

Every week it works out who actually showed up in your replies, likes and reposts, ranks the top hundred, and sends you the list as a digest. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. A week is short enough that the list still reflects who is engaging now rather than who did once, months ago.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @AdamLowisz shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
