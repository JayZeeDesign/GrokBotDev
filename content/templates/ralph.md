## What it does

Aimed at people whose best work does not survive being compressed into bullet points. It takes the CV you already have and builds it out into a portfolio site, producing Figma Make mockups, then setting Cursor agents on the build so each past project ends up as something a reader can open. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @HouseHackerJon shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
