## What it does

Pulls social-ready clips with karaoke-style captions and a source card, then drops the file straight onto your computer rather than leaving it in a cloud folder. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ThisWeeknAI shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
