## What it does

A report, a plan or a one-pager becomes a URL you can send, with no account needed at the other end. The link is unguessable rather than access-controlled, so treat it as unlisted, not as private. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @stevy_smith shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
