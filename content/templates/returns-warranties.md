## What it does

Pulls order confirmations out of Gmail and Drive, works out which purchases are worth tracking, and keeps an eye on the deadlines attached to them. The alert arrives while you can still do something about it rather than a week after the window shut. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
