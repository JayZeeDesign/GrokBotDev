## What it does

Support for the technical seller who has to respond in the moment while a prospect is still on the line. It supplies phrasing ready to say out loud, comparable deployments to cite when a point needs proof, quick lookups against the product docs, and motions tested in forward-deployed and customer-engineering work. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
