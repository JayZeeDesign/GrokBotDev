## What it does

A read-first layer over your inbox. It goes through what arrives, works out which messages look time-critical or have been sitting unanswered, and nudges you about those specifically. Sending, replying and deleting all stay in your hands unless you deliberately hand over more. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
