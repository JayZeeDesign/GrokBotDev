## What it does

Hooks into the Teslascope MCP so the history your car has already recorded becomes something you can simply ask about. Instead of reading dashboards, you put the question in ordinary language, whether that is how far you drove last month, which Superchargers you stopped at, or what state each vehicle is in. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @teslascope shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
