## What it does

It owns no work of its own. Every incoming task is handed to the specialist bot that should own it, and the hand-off is confirmed back to you rather than silently assumed. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
