## What it does

Goes through the pull requests sitting in your queue and sorts them into what can merge now, what still needs a review and what should simply be closed. The point is to arrive at a queue you can clear rather than a list you keep scrolling past. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @clairevo shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
