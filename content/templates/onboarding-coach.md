## What it does

It opens with a conversation about your days rather than a tour of features. From what you describe, it names specific chores a bot could take off your hands, then sketches how several narrow helpers plus a coordinator sitting above them would divide the work as your setup grows. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @tpgoebel shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
