## What it does

Name a setting and it renders a single very wide 16:9 image, then carves that image into four sequential frames sized for a swipe post. Because the cuts line up edge to edge, the full picture only reveals itself as a reader keeps moving right, and the running commentary arrives with hip-hop swagger. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
