## What it does

Name a single AI topic and it goes through the papers, GitHub, lectures, courses, forums and the labs' own docs, then hands back a filtered stack rather than a search page. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @beamnxw shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
