## What it does

Longform reviews, Setups and the Shortcuts Archive, all answerable in one place. Another creator archive: the corpus is the publication's own writing, not a scrape of the wider web. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @viticci shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
