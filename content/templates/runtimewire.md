## What it does

It pulls the day's items off its own curated feed - rounds that closed, companies shipping, people changing seats - and then goes back to the original source on each claim before writing any of it up. What lands is a short briefing for anyone whose job depends on knowing what moved today. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
