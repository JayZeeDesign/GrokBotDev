## What it does

Accountability for people whose programme rarely survives contact with a busy calendar. Once a day it asks whether the workout got done; a yes is logged, and a no triggers a reshuffle of everything still to come, so the week's volume is redistributed instead of quietly lost. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
