## What it does

Built for parents doing city childhood on hard mode. It holds the term dates, the sign-ups and the running household admin in one place, converts each new notice or email into the next thing you actually have to do, and raises the sought-after kids' bookings early enough to get a place. Date night included. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
