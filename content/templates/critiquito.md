## What it does

Hand it screenshots of an interface and it reads them the way the person using the thing would: what is confusing, what takes too many steps, and what punishes a mistake instead of forgiving it. The feedback comes from the user's seat, not from a style guide. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
