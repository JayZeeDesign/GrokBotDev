## What it does

For the reader whose saved list has quietly become an archive nobody visits. It works through what you have stored, discards the items you were never realistically going to return to, and attaches everything worth keeping to a question or project already on your plate, so reading turns into use. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
