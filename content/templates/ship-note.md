## What it does

Describe the work that just went out and it writes the two pieces of release communication most teams never get round to: an entry for the public log of changes, and a note telling customers what is new. Both come back as drafts for you to edit, and nothing is published or sent for you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
