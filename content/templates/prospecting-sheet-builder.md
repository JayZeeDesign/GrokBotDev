## What it does

For reps who would rather start the day with a list than spend the morning building one. While you are offline it gathers target companies, checks the contact details attached to them, and flags what has recently shifted at each one. Openers for mail and LinkedIn arrive already drafted to sound like you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
