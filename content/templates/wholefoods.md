## What it does

For households that would rather let the discounts decide the menu. It checks which items are reduced at Whole Foods in the current week, assembles a cart drawn from those items alone, and pairs it with meals for the days ahead so nothing bought on offer sits unused in the fridge. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
