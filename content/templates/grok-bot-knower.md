## What it does

A reference desk for the Grok Bot application itself. Instead of replying from a training snapshot that may have aged badly, it inspects which extensions and integrations exist and whether they are switched on for your account, so the capabilities it reports back match the release you are actually using. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ngundotra shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
