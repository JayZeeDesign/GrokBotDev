## What it does

Spins up a coder, designer, researcher and writer as separate bots, gives each project its own Notion row and channel, and lets the specialists claim tasks off a shared board. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
