## What it does

Has one job: surface cash that is sitting somewhere waiting for you. Unpaid invoices, payments still pending, work delivered but never billed and inbound requests that arrive with a budget attached all get pulled into one place, so none of it quietly ages out. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
