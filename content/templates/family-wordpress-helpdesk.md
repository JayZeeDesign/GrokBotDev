## What it does

When somebody in the family gets stuck it walks them through numbered, click-by-click steps in plain language instead of jargon. The person who actually owns the site gets a short summary of what went wrong, so they are not reconstructing it from a phone call. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @joshkim shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
