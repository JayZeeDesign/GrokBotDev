## What it does

Grok Bot already has bash on machines you have registered; this layers on the ability to see the screen and drive the interface rather than only the shell. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @brandon_ai shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
