## What it does

Built for gardeners in Britain who want to improve a plot they already keep rather than design a new one. It profiles where you live, how much sun the space gets, the ground you are working with and which plants are established there, then hands back the jobs worth doing in each part of the year. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
