## What it does

Point it at a template you are thinking of installing, or at one already running alongside you, and it goes looking for trouble: buried directives, permissions wider than the stated job needs, routines that fire with nobody watching. The verdict comes back as a plain approve or flag. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @GaurangKaria shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
