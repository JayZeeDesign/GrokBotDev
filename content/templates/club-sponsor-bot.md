## What it does

Starts by asking what the club is and what it needs, then works the outreach: companies that might sponsor in cash, employers who want campus recruiting access, and speakers worth inviting. Anyone who says no goes on a skip list so they are not chased twice. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
