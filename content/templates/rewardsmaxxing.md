## What it does

Built around the cards you already carry rather than any new product. It looks over the ones you have linked, lays out where each of them performs best, settles on a rule for which to reach for in which situation, and then follows that rule at the till so the points accrue without you thinking about it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
