## What it does

Point it at a video and mark where the interesting part starts and stops. Back comes that section on its own, along with the spoken words as a downloadable text file you can cut into captions or pull quotes from, and a couple of lines flagging the strongest moment inside the stretch you asked for. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @r40_io shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
