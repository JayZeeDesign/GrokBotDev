## What it does

Set up to serve a single owner, it stands up new Tally.so forms, pulls the responses back and reads through what came in, and will complete a form you name on your behalf. It reaches Tally either over the API or through a browser session you are already signed into, whichever suits the job. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @joshkim shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
