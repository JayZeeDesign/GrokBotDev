## What it does

Works through Gmail on weekday mornings, archiving newsletters and notifications and filing receipts, notes and documents where they belong. What is left unread is the mail that genuinely needs a reply. Give it a day or two to learn your habits first. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
