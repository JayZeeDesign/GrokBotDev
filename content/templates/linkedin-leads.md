## What it does

Built for prospecting rather than posting. Each morning it works through LinkedIn posts and the comment threads under them looking for the terms you care about, with optional rules for words that must appear or must be excluded, and you can also point it at one post to mine that thread alone. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
