## What it does

Collections of these things accumulate, and clearing one out usually means going and doing it yourself. Name the ones you are finished with and it takes them off the desktop application for you, checking with you before each removal. The point of it is that the deletion actually happens. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
