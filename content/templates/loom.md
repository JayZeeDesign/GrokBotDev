## What it does

Before writing anything it gathers the related threads, so the draft actually knows the history behind the question. Then it leaves the reply in front of you. It will not send, archive, delete or bin a single message, which makes it safe to point at a live inbox. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
