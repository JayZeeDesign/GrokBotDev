## What it does

A sparring partner for a founder holding an unproven idea. It reconstructs the beliefs the plan quietly depends on, gathers what supports each one and what argues against it, singles out the belief whose failure would end the whole thing, and names the cheapest experiment that would settle it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
