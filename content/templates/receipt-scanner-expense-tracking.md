## What it does

Built for people who mean to keep records but never open the shoebox. Give it a mailbox of its own, forward whatever arrives with a purchase on it, or send a single photo holding several paper slips, and each item lands as a row in your Google Sheet with the original saved to Drive under the month it belongs to. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
