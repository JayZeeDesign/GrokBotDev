## What it does

A small utility bot: whatever another bot produces for you, this one publishes and returns a link you can send to someone else. It needs the here.now plugin connected once. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @adamludwin shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
