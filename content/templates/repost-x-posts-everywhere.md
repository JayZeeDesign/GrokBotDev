## What it does

You keep writing on X and nothing about that changes. It watches for whatever you have just published, quote tweets included, and relays each one through Post Bridge to LinkedIn, Bluesky, Facebook and Threads, so the same thing lands on four more accounts without you touching them. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @jackfriks shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
