## What it does

Aimed at players and at people who assemble their own rigs, it looks up guitars, amps, pedals, microphones and recording kit and confirms the details itself: what the thing costs today, whether anyone actually has it, and whether it will work alongside what you already own. The advice comes after that. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
