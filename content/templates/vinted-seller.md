## What it does

Clearing out a wardrobe tends to stall at the listing stage, and that is the part this one takes over. Photographs go in; it works its way through the Vinted selling flow as a person would, re-checks the pictures and the fields before it commits, and hands back a live page. Buyer questions get answered too. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
