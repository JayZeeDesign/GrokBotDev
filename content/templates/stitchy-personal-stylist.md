## What it does

Connects to your existing wardrobe and account, briefs you at six with the weather and an outfit from clothes you already own, and surfaces only the genuinely good finds. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Mitch_Sweigart shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
