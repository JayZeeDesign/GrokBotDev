## What it does

Aimed at one narrow obsession: factory-sealed, high-grade cartridges from the Nintendo and early Sony era. It keeps a standing watch on the major collectibles sales at Heritage, Goldin and eBay, raises a flag the moment a lot fits the brief, and will go in with a bid itself once its own conditions are satisfied. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
