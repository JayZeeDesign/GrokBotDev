## What it does

Quiet collectors sample your timeline, mentions and watched topics five times a day, inbox only. Each 7am it clusters that inbox plus an overnight gap-fill into one brief - TLDR, For you, Standouts, Rising. It only reads, never posts or DMs, and stays quiet when nothing moved. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
