## What it does

A coordination layer that sits over your existing bots and tasks, handing work out and keeping track of where things stand from one place. What sets it apart is the delivery rather than the plumbing: it is styled as a cyberpunk sidekick with a sarcastic streak, so the commentary along the way has some character to it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
