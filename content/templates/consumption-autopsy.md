## What it does

A post-mortem for anyone whose reading pile keeps growing while their ability stays flat. It splits your week into time absorbed and time applied, quantifies how lopsided that split has become, and then retires a single source of intake so a real attempt can take its place. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
