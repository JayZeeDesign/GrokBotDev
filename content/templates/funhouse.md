## What it does

Turns the Grok Bot interface into something you decorate rather than merely use. It can repaint the surrounding shell, add animated companions and floating layers on top, and assemble themed novelties or one-off visual experiments purely for the look of the thing. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
