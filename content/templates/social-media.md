## What it does

The closing stage of a Portuguese-language Instagram line. Once artwork comes back from the design bot, it loads every carousel and standalone post into a publishing queue and releases them at hours that make sense in the audience's own time zone, so a full week can be lined up and then left to go out unattended. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
