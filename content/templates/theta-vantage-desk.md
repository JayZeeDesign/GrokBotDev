## What it does

Name a symbol and an expiry and it assembles the desk view sitting behind that trade: where dealers are positioned, how the contracts are changing hands, what volatility is currently pricing in, and which price levels matter. What comes back is a read to shape your own decision, not an order to place. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
