## What it does

If you run a site on Flowsery, this pulls out what its analysis already spotted while watching real visitors: the outright bugs, the flows that dead-end, and the interface friction that made people stumble. Everything comes back ordered by how many visitors actually hit it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @tarasshyn shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
