## What it does

Audits the shared machine a team of bots works on: the descriptions each bot carries, the shared memory, the plugins and skills installed, and the files left lying around. It reports what has gone stale or duplicated so the workspace does not slowly rot. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @chiplay shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
