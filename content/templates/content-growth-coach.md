## What it does

Point it at a channel, a profile or a stream and it reads the whole presence rather than one upload. Back comes a ranked answer: the thing limiting reach at present, the changes worth making in order of value, and a concrete idea for whatever you publish next. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @jxckvibe shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
