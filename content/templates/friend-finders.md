## What it does

Runs a quick sweep of your direct-message inbox, opens only the conversations that look like they matter, and comes back with a short list split by urgency. Aimed at people whose DMs have become a backlog they no longer read from top to bottom. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @pukerrainbrow shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
