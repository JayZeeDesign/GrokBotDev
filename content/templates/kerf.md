## What it does

Takes work that has already been sold and does the unglamorous part: deciding what is in scope and what is explicitly not, breaking the in-scope half into tickets, and then pushing each ticket until it is genuinely finished rather than nearly finished. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
