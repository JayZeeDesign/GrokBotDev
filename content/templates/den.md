## What it does

Households running several bots end up with one parent repeating the same context to each of them. This one takes the middle seat: it talks to a partner's bot and each child's bot on your behalf, so what you say in one place stays consistent everywhere else. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @poteto shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
