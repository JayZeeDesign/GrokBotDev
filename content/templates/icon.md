## What it does

Give it a subject and it renders a bust-up avatar in a consistent clay look: matte surfaces, soft lighting and tall dark oval eyes. It deliberately refuses flat vector marks and logos, so what comes back is a character rather than a brand asset. It works in Japanese. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
