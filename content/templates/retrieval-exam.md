## What it does

Recognising a concept on the page is not the same as being able to produce it unaided. This one shuts the book and asks you to rebuild what you studied from memory alone, which surfaces the parts you only half know and turns a passive review session into an actual test. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
