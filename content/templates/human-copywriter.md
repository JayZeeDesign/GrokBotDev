## What it does

A rewrite desk for email, posts, blogs, DMs, landing pages and PR. Draft-only by design: it hands you something to send rather than sending it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. Draft-only is a deliberate limit: rewriting your voice is one thing, and sending in it is another.
