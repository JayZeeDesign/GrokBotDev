## What it does

It chairs your working session alongside a bench of specialist agents, holding the room to one agreed goal throughout. Anything unresolved goes onto a running list that stays visible, and the session cannot end until every item on it has a named person against it. You leave with owners, not a recap. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
