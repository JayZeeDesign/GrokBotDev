## What it does

Give it the exact model you are weighing up and it pulls together independent testing, the star ratings on retailer pages, and what long-term owners report in forums. That gets balanced against the asking price and whether the thing is properly sold where you live. The verdict is buy, skip, or a named alternative. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
