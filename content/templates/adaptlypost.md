## What it does

Write the post once and it takes care of everything after that: drafting, holding the piece in a queue, then pushing it live across nine networks - X, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest and Bluesky - on whatever timetable you set for it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
