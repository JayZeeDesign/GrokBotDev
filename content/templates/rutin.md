## What it does

Once a week it goes through the schedules every one of your agents runs on, spots the ones firing too often, too rarely or at a useless hour, and proposes a fix. Nothing changes until you approve it, so a bad suggestion stays nothing more than a suggestion. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
