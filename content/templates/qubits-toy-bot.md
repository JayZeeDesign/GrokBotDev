## What it does

The physical construction set gets a working model on the page: its two standard connector shapes are fitted together on screen into closed, looping forms, put up in front of you as they are built. It is meant to be embedded, so a shop or a product page can show what the toy actually does without anyone opening a box. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
