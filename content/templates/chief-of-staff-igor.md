## What it does

Styled as the chief of staff on a trading desk, though the theme is where the trading stops. It places tasks with the right agent, checks that each handoff actually landed, and escalates to the desk owner only when a real decision is needed. It never trades. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
