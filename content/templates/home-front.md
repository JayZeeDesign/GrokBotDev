## What it does

Built for military families, it keeps watch over the VA side of life: where a claim currently stands, which visits are coming up, and what arrives in the post. When any of it needs a person, it says so rather than letting a date slip past. It also surfaces verified service-member discounts your household can use. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
