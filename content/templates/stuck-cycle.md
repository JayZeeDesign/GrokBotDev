## What it does

Takes a single skill and keeps it moving in a tight circuit: try something, hit the wall, then learn strictly the piece that the wall demands before going round again. Each lap finishes with a specific action small enough to start the same day, so momentum never depends on feeling prepared. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
