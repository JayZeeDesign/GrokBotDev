## What it does

One door for personal tasks: it opens rooms and creates the specialists each task needs, and it starts completely empty for every new owner rather than inheriting the author's life. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @TylerNishida shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
