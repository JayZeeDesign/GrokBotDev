## What it does

Captures wait times ahead of the trip and plans around your actual park days, whether you have Early Entry, and the rides you refuse to miss. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. Capturing the wait times ahead of the visit is what makes the plan real, because a park day built on averages falls apart by mid-morning.
