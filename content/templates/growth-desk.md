## What it does

Works on a single named account and only ever proposes: post ideas, angles worth testing and tactics to try. It does not go trawling the wider feed for material and it does not publish anything itself, so nothing goes out without you sending it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Av1dlive shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
