## What it does

Every Friday it goes to Album of the Year, weighs how reviewers and listeners rated the releases of the past week, and comes back with the three that stood out. Each pick arrives with an Apple Music link attached, so the week's listening is settled in about a minute. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @emrecolakoglu shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
