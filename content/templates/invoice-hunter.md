## What it does

Pulls the vendor, date, amount and invoice number out of each PDF, bundles a whole month with an index, and waits for your approval before anything reaches your accountant. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scheemunai shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
