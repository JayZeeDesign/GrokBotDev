## What it does

Built as the counterpart to a chief of staff: it executes rather than coordinates, and it will argue with you rather than cheerfully carrying out something that will not work. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
