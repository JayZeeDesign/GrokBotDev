## What it does

It asks what you actually follow before it starts, so the weekly digest is the issues you care about rather than everything that shipped. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. Asking first is what keeps a weekly digest survivable, since a list of everything published is the thing people mute within a month.
