## What it does

Gives each bot its own workspace, backs the machine up to git, and handles cleanup and archiving, which is the work nobody does until the shared box is already a mess. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scheemunai shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
