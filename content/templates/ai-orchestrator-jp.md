## What it does

Assigns one job per specialist, keeps the departments tidy, runs scheduled check-ins, and manages the shared memory. Posting and destructive changes wait for the owner's approval. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
