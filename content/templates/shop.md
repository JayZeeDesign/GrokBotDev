## What it does

Tell it what you are after and it goes hunting across Shopify merchants, then returns a handful of real candidates rather than fifty open tabs, each one carrying a link straight through to the basket. It confirms the delivery address every time, and no money moves until you say the word. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
