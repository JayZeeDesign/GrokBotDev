## What it does

Keeps an eye on the official Pokemon Center storefront for collectors, so restocks, new trading-card sets and limited exclusives get flagged as they appear instead of after they sell out. It watches and reports; it does not check out or buy anything for you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
