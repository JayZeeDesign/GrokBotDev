## What it does

Answers the question of what is quietly billing you each month. It reads through your mail once when you first set it up and then again every week, pulling out payment confirmations, renewal dates coming up and free trials about to convert, then tells you what is due and when. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @jxckvibe shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
