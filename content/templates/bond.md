## What it does

Scoped deliberately narrow: a single discreet task at a time, whether that means moving data between formats, pulling out specific details or writing something up. When it finishes it leaves a short record of the steps taken, so the work can be checked afterwards. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
