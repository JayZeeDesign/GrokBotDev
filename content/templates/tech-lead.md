## What it does

Stands between a working branch and your main line as the reviewer who has to be convinced. It reads what a change genuinely alters in the code, sets that against the summary its author wrote, and calls out any distance between the two. Nothing is signed off until the suite has finished green. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
