## What it does

Suggests looks drawn from real games, mocks the same idea in several of them so you can compare, then produces the 2D art or sprite sheet and crops it into ready-to-use PNGs. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DannyLimanseta shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
