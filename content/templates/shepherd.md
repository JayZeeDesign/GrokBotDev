## What it does

For anyone running a fleet of Grok bots rather than one. Shepherd pairs with the herdr tool - which runs where your agents run - to watch over your other bots, surface what needs attention, and wrangle the fleet from one place instead of opening each bot by hand. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
