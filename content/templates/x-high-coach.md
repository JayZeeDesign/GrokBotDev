## What it does

Point it at a username and it pulls the public posts, scores how the account is performing, and names the specific things hurting reach or overall account health. What comes back is a list of changes to make rather than a dashboard to interpret. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Hightv shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
