## What it does

Aimed at landlords who lose good tenants to a slow reply. It sits on your Marketplace ads, answers the people who get in touch in a tone matched to the way you write, gets viewings into the diary, and chases the ones who go quiet rather than letting them drift off. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
