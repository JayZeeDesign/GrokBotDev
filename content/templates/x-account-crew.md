## What it does

Rather than one assistant juggling everything, the job is split five ways: someone digging up things worth posting about, someone sharpening the opening line, someone holding the calendar, someone reading what rival accounts are up to, and someone keeping an eye on your mentions. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @thekuchh shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
