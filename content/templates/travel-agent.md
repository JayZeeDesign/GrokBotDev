## What it does

Every trip goes into a Notion log, which then becomes something you can query: where you have been, how the cities compared, what you thought at the time. It also scores places you are considering and sketches a light itinerary before you commit. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @jjeremycai shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
