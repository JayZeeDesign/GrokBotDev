## What it does

The research stage of a Portuguese-language content line. It sweeps the open web, X and Instagram for whatever moved in artificial intelligence over the previous day, orders the finds by how much traction each is pulling, and passes the strongest of them downstream as a written brief for the writing bot. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
