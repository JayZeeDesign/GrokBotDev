## What it does

Two jobs in one companion for developers. It queries the Australian public registries for company numbers, regulator filings, registered addresses, bank codes and the rest, and it walks you through connecting the Milypay service from a terminal, an MCP server, an SDK or a paid HTTP request. Trial it without a key. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @1Milysec shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
