## What it does

Forward it anything official that lands on the doormat or in the inbox. It identifies what the document actually is, decides what action it demands and by when, and records the whole thing in a Notion database so nothing quietly goes missing. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @liam_fallen shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
