## What it does

Opens a daily note for you, walks through the day's tasks first thing, and takes a spoken check-in at night. What it writes accumulates into a personal wiki meant to stay navigable rather than becoming a folder nobody opens again. It works in Portuguese. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
