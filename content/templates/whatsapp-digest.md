## What it does

Aimed at anyone who belongs to more group threads than they can follow. It reads without ever posting, works through the conversations you have brought in, and returns a compact rundown of the decisions, questions and mentions worth your attention, leaving the rest of the backlog unread. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @PetrusJvR shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
