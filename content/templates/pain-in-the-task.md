## What it does

Diagnosis comes first: working out which recurring chores, at the office or around the house, are quietly costing you the most hours. From there it makes the case for a particular sort of help for each of them, a bot or a routine or a changed process, and then goes ahead and puts it in place. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
