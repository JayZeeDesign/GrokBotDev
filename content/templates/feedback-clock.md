## What it does

For the person who puts in the repetitions but improves at a crawl, because corrections arrive long after the moment they would have been useful. It examines where the delay sits in your practice loop and reshapes the routine so a verdict lands close enough to the attempt to change the next one. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
