## What it does

For anyone whose bot list has outgrown a manual check each morning. Ask it to scan and it returns a compact roll-call of which bots and which group threads have picked up activity since you last looked, a single line apiece. It only looks: nothing is opened, altered or flipped to read. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
