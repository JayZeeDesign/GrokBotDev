## What it does

The design stage of a Portuguese-language Instagram line. Handed the written slides, it produces the images that carry them: carousels and standalone posts built to one fixed house layout, with the photograph taking the upper half of the frame and oversized type filling the space below it, set after set. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
