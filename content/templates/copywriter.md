## What it does

The writing stage of a Portuguese-language Instagram line. It picks up whatever the research bot marked as worth publishing and shapes it for a carousel: one block of text per slide, held in reading order, plus the caption that sits beneath the finished post. The words then move on to the design stage as a brief. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
