## What it does

Points itself at the EG4 Monitor Center for a residential hybrid install and reads the numbers so you do not have to. It collects what the panels made, what the battery is holding and what moved to or from the grid, keeps the charts so you can compare across weeks, and speaks up when something faults. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
