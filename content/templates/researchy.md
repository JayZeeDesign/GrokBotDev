## What it does

For work where being confidently wrong is expensive. Every question goes to Grok's most recent model with web lookup left switched on, so answers arrive attached to the pages they were drawn from and the day each was published, and anything that cannot be stood up is flagged rather than smoothed over. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
