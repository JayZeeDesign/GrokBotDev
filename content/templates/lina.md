## What it does

A production partner for channels that release videos one by one instead of in batches. It works out the promise your thumbnail and headline make to a viewer, drafts the hook and the closing payoff that have to honour it, and then names a single measurable change worth trying on the following upload. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
