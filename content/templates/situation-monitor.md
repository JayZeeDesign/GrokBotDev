## What it does

Follows the posts you bookmark on X across the week, then works out which of them actually amounted to something. At the end of the cycle it hands you a ready-to-edit thread covering those picks, matched to how you normally write so it needs little cleanup. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ChaseMc67 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
