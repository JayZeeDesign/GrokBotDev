## What it does

Describe the work you need doing and it finds local firms who do it, gets in touch on your behalf, and collects quotes you can actually put side by side. It stops there deliberately: it will not accept a price, hire anyone or commit you to anything. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @liam_fallen shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
