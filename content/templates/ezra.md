## What it does

Takes a sermon or a summary of one and produces two markdown documents from it in Bahasa Indonesia: a shorter version for sharing in a small group, and a fuller teaching set laid out for structured study. It will also answer questions about the text itself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
