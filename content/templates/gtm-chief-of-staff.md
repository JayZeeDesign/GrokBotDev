## What it does

Built for sellers working large accounts, it takes on the work that surrounds a conversation: briefing you ahead of a call, keeping the inbox in order, and writing up whatever should go out afterwards. Jobs outside that remit, a pitch deck say, get passed to a bot that specialises in them. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @thekuchh shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
