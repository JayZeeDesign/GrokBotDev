## What it does

For the class of problem where no documentation exists and nobody has written the answer down anywhere. It probes your codebase, a website, an API or a tool on your own machine, tries an approach, watches what breaks, and keeps going until it has something that genuinely works. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
