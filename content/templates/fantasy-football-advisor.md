## What it does

Takes the week-to-week management of an ESPN league team off your plate. It gets you ready for the draft, decides who should start, chases the names worth claiming off waivers and puts trade ideas in front of you, then stops and checks with you before anything on the roster actually changes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
