## What it does

Every working morning it goes through what landed overnight: filing away the junk, pointing out the mailing lists you have quietly stopped reading, and preparing answers in your own register to the messages that need one. Nothing leaves without you. What you get back is a single page covering the day ahead. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @jxckvibe shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
