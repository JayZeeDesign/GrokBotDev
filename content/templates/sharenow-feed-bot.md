## What it does

Give it a topic and it tracks Instagram, X, Threads, YouTube and TikTok every hour onto a board that stays current. It needs a third-party ShareNow connection to publish. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @sharenow_today shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
