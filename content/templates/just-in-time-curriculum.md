## What it does

Built for the learner whose preparation never quite ends. Rather than maintaining a queue of courses to get through first, it looks at the concrete task in front of you, isolates the single thing you must understand to finish it, and leaves the rest of the syllabus unopened until a job calls for it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
