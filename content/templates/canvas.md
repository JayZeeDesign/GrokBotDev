## What it does

It signs into your university portal in the browser to read your units and deadlines, so be aware it is holding a session on your student account. It helps you work through assignments rather than doing them. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @daxperera shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
