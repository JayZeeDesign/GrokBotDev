## What it does

A cost overseer for anyone running more than a couple of bots against Cursor. It keeps a running picture of where the budget is going, identifies the individual bots responsible for the largest share of it, and proposes changes that trim the bill while leaving the work they produce intact. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
