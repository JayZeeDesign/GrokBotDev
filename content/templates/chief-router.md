## What it does

It never does the work. It picks the single bot that owns each job and then goes quiet, pinging you only when something has shipped, stalled, or needs a decision only you can make. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
