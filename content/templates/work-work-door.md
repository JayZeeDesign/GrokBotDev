## What it does

Spawns its own channels and specialists as the work arrives, and deliberately remembers nothing from the person who built it, so a clone starts clean instead of pre-loaded. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @TylerNishida shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
