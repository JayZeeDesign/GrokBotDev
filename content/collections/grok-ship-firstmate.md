This is the designated launch pairing: one builder's progression from a chief Bot that manages other Bots to a full software factory with state, delegation, review and cloud agents behind it.

Start with the captain, then ship with the crew. Firstmate is the control pattern, and it is the one you can understand — and copy — in an afternoon: one Bot that classifies work, routes it, tracks the handoff by task ID, and refuses to hold your secrets. Grok Ship is what that pattern turns into once it has a backlog, per-project crewmates, adversarial review and a rule that no pull request merges without you.

Read them in that order and the second one stops looking like magic. The same charter is doing the work in both; Grok Ship just gives it a database, a crew and somewhere to put the results.
