X is now a first-class Grok Bot plugin: a Bot can read the timeline, follow accounts, watch mentions and post, without a scraper or a third-party bridge in the middle. That is raw access. It is not, on its own, a run account.

This is the crew that turns one into the other. Five Bots, each shared publicly by someone already doing the job, and each pointed at a different part of the week. The Account Crew is the hub — five roles inside one bot, so somebody owns finding the material, sharpening the line, holding the calendar, reading rivals and watching mentions. The Strategist works the slow half nobody schedules: which of the accounts in your replies are worth an actual relationship. The Brief reads your own posting history to work out what your beat is, then reports back on what moved in it. High Coach is the honest one — point it at any public account, yours or a competitor's, and it scores the thing and names what to change. Repost Everywhere closes the loop, relaying whatever you just published out to four other networks.

Start with the Account Crew and add the rest as the gaps show up. Each one installs on its own, so this is a shopping list rather than a stack you have to commit to — and every one of them is somebody else's setup, copied into your account, which is exactly why the instructions are worth reading before you add them.
