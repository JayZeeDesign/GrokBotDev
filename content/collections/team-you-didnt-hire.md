A startup's first hires are always the same handful of roles: someone to run the calendar, someone to sell, someone to market, someone to write, and someone to watch the burn. This is that team, minus the salaries, the onboarding and the notice periods.

Five bots, each pointed at a role you would normally pay for — executive assistant, CMO, SDR, ops, copywriter — so a company of one can run like a company of six. Every one installs on its own, so treat it as a hiring plan rather than a package: add the roles you are actually missing, in the order the gaps hurt.
